What Makes a Password Safe?

Move beyond weak single-words to multi-layered credentials.

📏

Length

Aim for 12+ characters. Length exponentially increases cracking difficulty far more than complexity alone.

🧩

Complexity

Mix uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special symbols (e.g., !@#$%).

🗣️

Passphrases

Combine 4 random words into a sentence. Easy for humans to remember, hard for computers to guess.

Common Password Mistakes

Habits that put your identity and finances at risk.

🔄 Reusing Passwords

Using the same password for email, social media, and ePayslip means one leak compromises all accounts.

📝 Sticky Notes

Writing credentials on paper attached to monitors or hidden under keyboards.

📅 Personal Dates

Using birth dates, names of children, or local sports teams that can be found on public social profiles.

🤝 Sharing Credentials

Sending passwords over messaging apps, email, or sharing accounts with colleagues.

Forgotten Password?

Follow the official self-service password reset procedure.

1️⃣

Request Reset

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page. Enter your official employee ID/email.

2️⃣

Verify Identity

Check your official inbox/SMS for a single-use verification link or OTP code.

3️⃣

Set New Password

Create a fresh, unique passphrase. Never reuse your previous 3 passwords.

5 Things to Remember

Quick recap for your daily digital security routine.

  • ✓ Use long, unique passphrases for every official account.
  • ✓ Never write passwords down or share them with colleagues.
  • ✓ Always check the URL before logging into the ePayslip portal.
  • ✓ Log out of your ePayslip account when using shared computers.
  • ✓ Report suspicious password reset requests to IT immediately.
